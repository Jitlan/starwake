How to hold a rock

Field manual

  1. The engineers repair the ship one wave at a time. Hold every wave at a site and it lifts off to the next rock. Five rocks and you are home.
  2. Your hand is four cards from your deck. Tap a card, then tap a tile, or drag the card onto the rock. Energy refills during waves and when you clear one.
  3. Aliens float straight at the ship and chew through anything in their way. Red chevrons show where the next wave comes from.
  4. Alien fire hits the first defense in its path. Walls soak it up, Interceptors shoot bullets out of the air and Shield Domes absorb them until drained.
  5. Defenses that fall are gone for good. Patch Kits, Overclocks, supply picks and Repair Bays keep them standing.
  6. After each wave, pick one of three supplies. Between rocks, spend scrap at the refit.
  7. Lose the ship and the run ends. Every card you unlock stays unlocked for the next run.
  8. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a defense to inspect it. 2× speed appears during waves.
